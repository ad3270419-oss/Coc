Clash Planner: Real-Time Clash of Clans Upgrade & Boost Tracker
Clash Planner is an offline-first companion app built for Clash of Clans players who want complete control over their builder schedules and potion efficiency.
One-Click JSON Sync: Import your village export file to instantly populate live upgrade timers, helper boosts, and builder states without manual typing.  
Smart Potion & Boost Engine: Accurately calculates accelerated completion times for Builder (10x), Research (24x), and Pet potions.  
Multi-Village Isolation: Switch between multiple player accounts seamlessly with locked in-game player tag security.  
Hardware-Accurate Alerts: Persistent, restart-safe local notifications alert you the second an upgrade completes, keeping your progression loop continuous.  
Monthly Efficiency Stats: Track cumulative days and hours saved each month across Builders, Laboratory, and Pet House.
